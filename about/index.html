Who we are.

With more than 30 years of combined experience, we develop residential and mixed-use projects, from the first look at a plot to the handover of the keys. Our current projects are Mareterra in Glyfada and Sachtouri 44–46 in Chalandri. V5 Residences in Neo Psychiko was completed in January 2025 and sold out.

The same people stay with each project from start to finish. We design every home around light, space and the way people actually live, and we build it faithfully to the drawings, with materials chosen to last.

How we work.

Every project starts by listening: how our buyers live, and the place they want to live in.

  1. Listening first

    Before we draw, we learn how our buyers live and what they need from a home.

  2. Space, reimagined

    Every home has its own floor plan, with bespoke storage designed into it from the start.

  3. Natural light as the rule

    Orientation, openings and layout are decided around daylight.

  4. Materials you can touch

    Tactile interiors in stone, wood and quality finishes that age well.

  5. A++ in every project

    Every project we build is rated energy class A++.
