Five homes, five different plans.

Sachtouri 44–46 is a new residential building in Chalandri, in the northern suburbs of Athens. Each of its five homes is designed around a different concept, from a 62 m² first-floor apartment to a 138 m² maisonette with a private rooftop pool.

The architecture is contemporary and minimal: earthy and grey tones, natural materials and large windows that bring daylight deep into every room. The main living spaces have high ceilings. Every home is delivered with new kitchens, bathrooms and installations, a heat pump and underfloor heating, rated energy class A++.

Outdoors is treated as part of the home. Spacious terraces and large openings let daylight reach every corner, and planted terraces soften the city around them. On the top floor, the maisonette has its own 42 m² rooftop with a swimming pool, for unwinding and hosting in full privacy.
