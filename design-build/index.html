One team, from the first line to the keys.

We design and build every GP building ourselves. Our in-house architect draws each home, the same team takes it through permits and construction, and we stay with you after handover. If you own a plot and want to build on it, we can do the same for you.

Why one team.

  1. One responsibility

    The people who draw the building are the people who build it. No hand-offs between an architect’s office and a contractor, and one point of contact for you from the first meeting to the keys.

  2. Every home, its own floor plan

    Our in-house architect designs each residence on its own instead of repeating one plan across the building, starting from light, orientation and storage.

  3. A++ from the start

    Every project we build is rated energy class A++: a heat pump for heating and cooling, underfloor heating and high insulation, decided at the drawing stage, not added later.

How a GP building is made.

The same five steps, whether the building is ours or yours.

  1. First meeting

    We listen first: who will live there, how, and what the plot allows. You get a plain answer about what can be built.

  2. Design

    Our in-house architect designs every home individually, around light, orientation, storage and the way a family actually lives.

  3. Permits

    We take the design through the permits, so you deal with one team instead of several offices.

  4. Construction

    The same team builds what it drew, with materials chosen to age well: stone, wood and quality fittings from Greek and European producers.

  5. Handover

    We walk you through every system of the home and remain the people you call afterwards.

Design & Build, answered plainly.

What owners ask us before they build.

What is design & build?

Design & build means that one company handles both the design and the construction of a building. At GP, our in-house architect designs the building and the same team takes it through permits, construction and handover, so you have one point of contact from the first drawing to the keys.

Can GP design and build on my own plot?

Yes. Tell us where the plot is and what you would like to build, and we will tell you plainly what can be built there. If you would rather not fund the construction yourself, antiparochi may suit you better.

Who designs the homes?

Our in-house architect. Each home is designed on its own, instead of one floor plan repeated across the building.

Which energy class do your buildings reach?

Every project we build is rated energy class A++, with a heat pump, underfloor heating and high insulation.

Where do you build?

Mainly in the northern suburbs of Athens and in Glyfada. Our buildings so far are in Neo Psychiko, Chalandri and Glyfada.
