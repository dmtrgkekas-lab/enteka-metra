How we build a GP home.

Five things that stay the same in every building we make.

  • Our in-house architect designs each residence individually instead of repeating one plan across the building. We start from light, orientation and storage, and design around the way a family actually lives.

  • Stone, wood and quality sanitary ware from Greek and European producers. We choose materials that age well, so the home still feels right decades from now.

  • Every project is rated energy class A++: a heat pump for heating and cooling, underfloor heating and high insulation. At Mareterra we added net metering photovoltaics, a 12 cm thermal façade and EV charging.

  • Large, well-oriented terraces, gardens on the ground floor and private pools in selected maisonettes and penthouses. We design the outdoor space with the same care as the living room.

  • From buying the plot to handing over the keys, the same people design, build and keep you informed. After handover we walk you through every system of the home and remain the people you call.

Ask us directly
Which developer builds new homes in Neo Psychiko, Chalandri and Glyfada?

GP Real Estate Development, a family real estate development and investment company based in Psychiko, Athens, with more than 30 years of combined experience. Its projects are V5 Residences in Neo Psychiko (sold out), Sachtouri 44–46 in Chalandri and Mareterra in Glyfada.

Are there new-build apartments for sale in Chalandri?

Yes. Sachtouri 44–46 is a new A++ building in the centre of Chalandri with apartments and two maisonettes, each designed around its own concept. Every home has a heat pump, underfloor heating and new kitchens and bathrooms; the top-floor maisonette has a private rooftop pool.

What homes does Mareterra in Glyfada offer?

Mareterra, at Saki Karagiorga 77 & Lykourgou, is 250 metres from the Glyfada Golf Course. It has 192 m² maisonettes with a private garden and pool, 162 m² duplexes with sea views and 144 m² duplex penthouses with a 40 m² rooftop and private pool. Every home has a two-car garage and storage.

What does energy class A++ mean for a new home?

A++ is the top rating of the Greek energy performance certificate (PEA). In practice it means very low energy needs for heating and cooling. Every GP project is rated A++ and uses heat pumps, underfloor heating and high insulation.

How does antiparochi work with GP?

In antiparochi the landowner contributes the plot and we cover the design, permits and construction. When the building is finished, the landowner receives an agreed share, usually as finished apartments. We work mainly in the northern suburbs of Athens and in Glyfada, and we also buy land outright.

Can I buy a home in Athens from abroad?

Yes. We work with buyers abroad in English and Greek, hold online meetings and guide you through the steps of the purchase together with your lawyer.

Do you work with real estate agents?

Yes. Agents can send us their client’s request through the form on this page, and we reply with floor plans and availability for our current projects.
