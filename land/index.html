Antiparochi, answered plainly.

What is antiparochi?

Antiparochi is the Greek arrangement in which a landowner contributes a plot to a developer, and in exchange receives an agreed share of the finished building, usually as apartments, without paying for the construction.

How does antiparochi work with GP?

You contribute the plot and we cover the design, the permits and the construction. When the building is finished, you receive an agreed share, usually as finished apartments.

Do you also buy land?

Yes. Besides antiparochi, we also buy land outright.

In which areas are you looking for plots?

Mainly in the northern suburbs of Athens and in Glyfada.
