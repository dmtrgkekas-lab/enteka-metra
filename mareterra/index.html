Between the golf course and the sea.

Mareterra is a five-storey residential building at Saki Karagiorga 77 and Lykourgou, in the golf area of Glyfada. It holds six homes in three types: two maisonettes with a private garden and pool, two duplexes with sea views, and two duplex penthouses with a private rooftop pool.

The Glyfada Golf Course is 250 metres away and Ellinikon Park is a short walk. The building is rated energy class A++, with net metering photovoltaics, a 12 cm thermal façade, underfloor heating and EV charging in the underground garage.
