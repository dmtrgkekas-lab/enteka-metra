Earthy lines in Neo Psychiko.

V5 Residences is a residential building at Voutieridou 5, in the centre of Neo Psychiko, with quick access to central Athens. Its nine homes range from two studios on the first floor to a penthouse across the 8th and 9th floors.

The design follows minimalism through the lens of green building: dynamic lines in earthy tones, large surfaces and a balanced microclimate inside each home. Materials come from Greek and European producers. Every resident has a parking space and a large storage room, and the upper floors look out over Athens.

Completed in January 2025, V5 Residences sold in full, from the first-floor studios to the penthouse. The same family team now builds Mareterra in Glyfada and Sachtouri 44–46 in Chalandri.
